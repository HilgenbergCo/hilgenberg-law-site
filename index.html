Hilgenberg Law LLC

Real Estate, Land Use, Development, and Conservation Counsel

Overview

Hilgenberg Law LLC provides legal counsel on real estate transactions, land use and zoning matters, development approvals, conservation planning, property rights, regulatory, and business matters. Owner Scott Hilgenberg operates his multi-state practice from Green Bay, WI, serving clients in Wisconsin, Oregon, and Washington.

Scott Hilgenberg works with property owners, developers, investors, businesses, local governments, and nonprofit organizations on matters involving property development, land use planning, and long-term stewardship. Drawing on experience in private practice, government service, and commercial real estate, Scott helps clients evaluate opportunities, manage risk, and achieve practical business and ownership objectives.

  • Land Use, Zoning, and Development
  • Conservation and Land Stewardship
  • Easements, Access, Title, and Property Rights
  • Regulatory and Administrative Matters
  • Business Formation, Contracts, and Transactions

Attorney and Law Firm Support

Scott contracts with attorneys and law firms requiring specialized support on land use, development, real estate, conservation, regulatory, and property rights matters. Services include strategic consultation, permitting, research and document drafting, due diligence, and assistance with complex development and property-related issues.

About Scott

Scott earned a Bachelor of Arts in Legal Studies from the University of Wisconsin-Madison and a Juris Doctor from Lewis & Clark Law School, where he also earned a Certificate in Environmental and Natural Resources Law.

His background includes government service, conservation-related work, commercial real estate brokerage, and property development. He has advised property owners, developers, businesses, financial institutions, public interest organizations, and nonprofit organizations on land use, real estate, and business matters.

Scott has substantial experience in land use litigation, permitting, and public policy, including service as Staff Attorney for the Oregon Land Use Board of Appeals and Chair of the Oregon State Bar's Real Estate and Land Use Section.

Scott is licensed to practice law in Wisconsin, Oregon, and Washington.
