Overview

Hilgenberg Law LLC provides legal counsel for real estate, land use, development, conservation, business, and succession planning matters.

Scott Hilgenberg works with landowners, developers, businesses, and families on matters involving property, growth, planning, and long-term stewardship. Drawing on experience in private practice, government service, commercial real estate, conservation, and in-house counsel roles, Scott helps clients evaluate opportunities, manage risk, and achieve practical business and ownership objectives.

Specialty Counsel for Law Firms

Scott contracts with law firms requiring specialized support on land use, development, real estate, conservation, regulatory, and property rights matters. He assists attorneys and their clients with complex projects, permitting efforts, property issues, and matters benefiting from focused subject-matter experience.

About Scott

Scott earned a Bachelor of Arts in Legal Studies from the University of Wisconsin-Madison and a Juris Doctor from Lewis & Clark Law School, where he also earned a Certificate in Environmental and Natural Resources Law.

His background includes private practice, government service, conservation-related work, commercial real estate brokerage, and land development. He has advised landowners, developers, businesses, financial institutions, and public interest organizations on property, development, land use, and business matters. Scott has extensive experience in land use litigation and policy, including public service as staff attorney for Oregon's Land Use Board of Appeals and chair of the Oregon State Bar's Real Estate and Land Use Section.

Scott is licensed to practice law in Wisconsin, Oregon, and Washington. Outside of his practice, he enjoys hiking, biking, and spending time with family lakeside.
