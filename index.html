Hilgenberg Law LLC

Real Estate, Land Use, Conservation and Development Counsel

Hilgenberg Law LLC provides legal counsel on real estate, land use and zoning, development, conservation planning, property rights, regulatory issues, and business matters in Wisconsin, Oregon, and Washington. Owner Scott Hilgenberg maintains his multi-state real estate and land use practice from Green Bay, Wisconsin. His experience includes working with property owners, businesses, governments, and organizations on matters involving the use, development, acquisition, management, conservation, and long-term stewardship of real property.

  • Real estate transactions and development
  • Land use, zoning, and development approvals
  • Oregon Land Use Board of Appeals (LUBA) matters and land use appeals
  • Property rights and regulatory matters
  • Conservation planning and real property stewardship
  • Business and real estate counsel
